Solvinter Edge

Phase 02 — Mumford Learning Space

Why Mumford

The second Solvinter Edge node is located in Mumford, Ghana.

While the Accra node explores economic opportunity through digital work, the Mumford node explores how local AI can strengthen education, creativity and community development.

We do not begin with technology.

We begin with learning.

The objective is to create a place where children, teachers, entrepreneurs and creators can explore new possibilities together.

The Learning Space

The Mumford node is centred around a dedicated learning environment.

It is designed as a calm, aesthetically pleasing space where people can learn, create and collaborate.

Technology is only one part of the environment.

The space itself should encourage curiosity, creativity and lifelong learning.

Three Functions

School Learners
  • homework support
  • reading and writing
  • science exploration
  • computing
  • AI-assisted tutoring
  • independent learning
  • educational resources
Entrepreneurs
  • business planning
  • budgeting
  • agricultural planning
  • AI-assisted research
  • digital design
  • technical mentoring
  • online services
Maker Space
  • playful exploration
  • 3D printing
  • robotics
  • electronics
  • programming
  • game development
  • digital art
  • animation
  • music production
  • video creation
  • AI-assisted creativity
  • rapid prototyping

Eira

The first educational platform developed within Solvinter Edge is Eira.

Eira is an open-source educational AI designed to operate locally, allowing schools to continue using AI even when Internet connectivity is unreliable.

Rather than replacing teachers, Eira is intended to become another educational resource available to schools and communities.

The Pilot

The first pilot will be conducted in one primary school in Mumford.

Initially the project will collaborate with one headteacher, one classroom teacher and one Upper Primary classroom (Basic 4–6).

The first subject will be Mathematics before gradually expanding to additional subjects.

Local Infrastructure

  • local AI server
  • NVIDIA GPU workstation
  • UPS backup
  • local Wi-Fi
  • optional Starlink connectivity
  • curriculum knowledge base
  • local document retrieval

Educational knowledge remains available locally through Retrieval- Augmented Generation (RAG), reducing dependence on continuous Internet access.

Principles

Teacher First

Teachers remain central to education. AI should support rather than replace professional educators.

Local First

Educational infrastructure should continue functioning even when the Internet does not.

Human-AI Collaboration

Human expertise and artificial intelligence should complement one another.

Open Source

Educational technology should be transparent, reproducible and openly shared whenever possible.

Solvinter Edge explores how local educational infrastructure can expand opportunity through learning, creativity and artificial intelligence.

Solvinter Edge

Phase 02 — Mumford Learning Space

Why Mumford

The second Solvinter Edge node is located in Mumford, Ghana.

While the Accra node explores economic opportunity through digital work, the Mumford node explores how local AI can strengthen education, creativity and community development.

We do not begin with technology.

We begin with learning.

The objective is to create an inspiring place where children, teachers, entrepreneurs and creators can explore new possibilities together.

The Learning Space

The Mumford node is centred around a dedicated learning environment.

Unlike the Accra node, this building is designed primarily as a place for education, creativity and community development.

Eira

The first educational platform developed within Solvinter Edge is Eira.

Eira is an open-source educational AI designed to operate locally, allowing schools to continue using AI even when Internet connectivity is unreliable.

Rather than replacing teachers, Eira is intended to become another educational resource that supports learning both inside and outside the classroom.

The Pilot

The first pilot will be conducted in one primary school in Mumford.

Initially the project will collaborate with one headteacher, one classroom teacher and one Upper Primary classroom (Basic 4–6).

The pilot will begin with Mathematics before gradually expanding to additional subjects and learning resources.

Local Infrastructure

Eira is designed as a local-first educational platform.

  • local AI server
  • NVIDIA GPU workstation
  • UPS backup power
  • local Wi-Fi network
  • optional Starlink connectivity
  • curriculum knowledge base
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Educational materials remain available locally, reducing dependence on continuous Internet access while allowing schools to build their own knowledge base over time.

Principles

Teacher First

Teachers remain central to education. AI should support rather than replace professional educators.

Local First

Educational infrastructure should continue functioning even when Internet connectivity is unavailable.

Human–AI Collaboration

Human intelligence and artificial intelligence should complement one another to improve learning outcomes.

Open Source

Educational technology should be transparent, reproducible and openly shared whenever possible so that communities can adapt and improve it.

Looking Forward

The Mumford Learning Space is intended to become a model for how local educational infrastructure can combine teachers, communities and open artificial intelligence.

If successful, the experience gained in Mumford will guide future Solvinter Edge learning spaces in other communities.

Solvinter Edge explores how local educational infrastructure can expand opportunity through learning, creativity and artificial intelligence.
